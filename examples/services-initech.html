01 — The situation

Three companies on one cap table, still running like three companies.

Initech completed the Veridian and OpenSphere acquisitions 11 months ago. The legal entities are merged. The operating model isn't. P&L responsibility is unclear at the segment level, three separate IT estates duplicate $4.2M annually, and the executive team has spent the last quarter relitigating decisions that should belong to single owners.

You've identified the symptoms. What you need is a defensible target operating model, owned at the board level, that can survive a 12-month implementation through the operational and political resistance you're already seeing.

03 — Our approach

Diagnostic. Design. Decision.

A 14-week engagement structured to converge on a board-ready answer. Two senior partners on every workstream — no associate-led delivery. We will refuse to do PowerPoint theatre. The output is a model your CFO can defend.

01

Diagnostic · Weeks 1–4

Decision-rights audit (37 material decisions, ~80 stakeholder interviews), cost duplication mapping, P&L attribution analysis, customer-facing role overlap. Output: a current-state document with synergy quantification ranges.

Why this first: Most failed integrations design the future state without understanding the current one. We won't.

02

Design · Weeks 5–10

Target operating model, segment P&L structure, decision-rights matrix (RACI on the 37 decisions), shared-services blueprint, governance forums and cadence. Iterated through three executive working sessions.

Why now: Design without diagnostic is theatre. Design without iteration with the people who must execute it never lands.

03

Decision & plan · Weeks 11–14

Board-ready recommendation, 12-month implementation roadmap with named owners and quarterly milestones, change-management plan, internal communication assets.

Why last: The plan only writes after the design is endorsed by your top 8. Otherwise you're planning a fight, not an implementation.

08 — Risks & how we handle them

What could go wrong

Risk

Top-8 alignment fractures during design phase

Operating-model redesigns surface political fault lines. Some surface late.

Mitigation

Bi-weekly working sessions with documented dissent register. Disagreements escalated to CEO with options memo within 5 business days. No surprises in the board package.

Risk

Diagnostic interviews stall

If we can't get 80 hours of stakeholder time in weeks 1–4, the foundation slips.

Mitigation

Your COO chief of staff blocks calendars in week 0. We'll send the schedule to the top-8 with COO endorsement. Missed sessions cost the project, not us.

Risk

Synergy estimates inflate beyond defensibility

Pressure to justify the deal can push synergy numbers past honest ranges.

Mitigation

All synergy numbers carry confidence levels (high/medium/low) and are bottom-up modeled. We will not deliver a number we can't defend to your audit committee.
