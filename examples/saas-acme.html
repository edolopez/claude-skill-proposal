03 — Our approach

Three phases. The first ships in 10 days.

We deploy inside your existing Salesforce instance — no migrations, no new tab for AEs to forget about. The model trains on your closed-won/lost data, then surfaces signals where the work already happens.

01

Calibration · Weeks 1–2

Connect to Salesforce, ingest 24 months of opportunity history, train the close-probability model on your data shape. Output: a baseline accuracy report you can compare against today's forecast.

Why this first: If the model isn't 75%+ accurate on historical data, we tell you before you commit. No surprises in week 6.

02

In-Salesforce surfacing · Weeks 3–6

Native Lightning components on the opportunity record: close-probability gauge, next-best-action prompts, deal-risk flags. AEs see the signal at the exact moment they update the deal.

Why now: Behavior change requires the signal to be in the workflow, not adjacent to it. This is the difference vs. Gong/Clari.

03

Manager rollup + ramp · Weeks 7–10

Forecast roll-up dashboard for sales leaders, weekly accuracy report, and AE coaching cadence playbook for your enablement team.

Why last: Manager tooling is only useful once AEs are using it daily. Ship the leaf, then the branch.

08 — Risks & how we handle them

What could go wrong

Three honest risks. Each has a mechanism, not just a hope.

Risk

If your historical CRM hygiene is poor, the model may not exceed 75% accuracy.

Mitigation

Week-2 calibration gate. If accuracy is below 75%, you can exit with 50% refund — we keep only what's been earned.

Risk

AE adoption stalls below 60%

In-Salesforce surfacing reduces this risk vs. dashboards, but it's still possible.

Mitigation

Weekly adoption reports during 90-day ramp. If we see <60% rep usage at day 60, we co-fund a 2-week intervention with your enablement team — no extra cost.

Risk

Mitigation
